Venue Dimensions & Technical Specifications

Constructed with pillarless sightlines, the Grand Imperial Ballroom allows all 1,500 guests an uninterrupted view of the celebration stage. The acoustics are engineered with custom sound-absorbing wall panels to eliminate echo.
