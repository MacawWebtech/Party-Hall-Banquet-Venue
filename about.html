The Regalia Heritage

742 Bellevue Avenue, Newport, Rhode Island 02840, United States.

Established 1912

From An American Gilded Age Vision to a Monument of Celebrations

Regalia was conceived with a single resolute aspiration: to provide families with a sanctuary where momentous occasions are celebrated with imperial dignity and genuine warmth.

Situated along Bellevue Avenue among Newport's historic summer palaces, Regalia encompasses forty-five thousand square feet of stone architecture, Italian marble balustrades, and oceanfront breeze.

Purpose

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be America's most treasured home for life's grandest celebrations

We see Regalia as a living landmark, where heritage architecture and modern hospitality come together so that every family gathers beneath the same legacy of grandeur.

  • Preserve the Gilded Age estate for future generations
  • Set the benchmark for luxury venue hospitality
  • Make every celebration a lasting family memory
Our Mission

To deliver imperial dignity with genuine warmth, one celebration at a time

Through attentive planning, exceptional cuisine and flawless service, we turn each guest's vision into an occasion that feels effortless and unforgettable.

  • A dedicated concierge from first tour to final toast
  • Farm-to-table dining crafted by our culinary team
  • Transparent pricing and seamless event logistics
