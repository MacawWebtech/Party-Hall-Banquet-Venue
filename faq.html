Reservation & Venue FAQs

Everything you need to know about celebrating your auspicious milestones at Regalia.

You can start by submitting an online date enquiry through our 9-step booking wizard. Our senior concierge will verify date exclusivity on our master ledger and issue an official reservation memorandum. A 25% advance booking fee secures the hall and date exclusively for your family.

Payments are structured into four transparent phases:
1. 25% Advance upon reservation confirmation.
2. 35% Second installment 60 days prior to the celebration.
3. 30% Third installment 15 days prior following final menu tasting.
4. 10% Final settlement on the conclusion of the event day.

We understand family emergencies. Date rescheduling requests received more than 90 days before the scheduled event are transferred to any mutually agreeable available date within 12 months with zero penalty fee. Cancellations received beyond 120 days receive an 80% refund of the advance deposit.

Regalia is equipped with dual 500 kVA Cummins acoustic silent generators with automatic synchronizing panels. Power transitions take less than 1.5 seconds, ensuring uninterrupted air conditioning, stage lighting, and audio.

Yes, external floral architects and specialized lighting designers are welcome upon signing our venue safety and structural preservation guidelines. We also offer fully curated in-house packages for seamless coordination.

We provide dedicated multi-level parking for 450+ vehicles. Every grand wedding and reception booking includes professional valet parking with RFID retrieval tickets.
